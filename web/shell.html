Wren's notebook

Familiar

Halloween night, 2026, in the hill town of Hollowell. The sun is setting where it really sets at 42° north. Choose a costume and go trick-or-treating: knock on doors where the porch light is on, and have a joke ready. And tonight you can turn into a black cat: small and quick, able to jump onto fences, sheds and even roofs, slip through gaps, and nose up a latch from the inside. A cat can't knock or carry the candy bag, so the bag waits where you changed. And a cat sees the spirits out walking on the one night the veil is thin: the town's own dead, visiting.

The cat and the spirits are this game's fairy tale, not something Wiccans believe or do.

  • Mouse: look. WASD: walk, Shift: hurry.
  • 1–4 on Nana's walk: choose a costume. E: knock, pick up your bag, or open a latch. T: tell a joke when a neighbour asks for a trick. Q: turn into a cat, and back.
  • E also lights a lantern, picks an apple or rosemary, buys at Abbas's Market, stirs Nana's pot and makes the offering. Tab: Wren's notebook (← → turn its pages on a desktop).
  • F1: the controls on screen. F5: save, F9: load the last save. Esc: free the mouse.
  • Drag on the left half to walk (all the way out to hurry); drag on the right half to look.
  • Choose a costume with the buttons on Nana's walk. Knock at a door; Tell a joke when asked for a trick, or Just the treat; Cat turns you into a cat and Be Wren back. The big button changes to Pick up by your bag and Open at a latch.
  • It also changes with what is in reach: Light a lantern, Pick an apple or rosemary, Buy at the market, Stir the pot, Offer at the stones; at the market, short of candy, Offer a joke. Notebook (top) opens Wren's notebook.
  • Tap the words at the top left to fold them away. Save saves now (the game also saves by itself); New asks before starting the night over.

Tonight Nana keeps Samhain, and you help: light the four family lanterns, brew her cider, find Smoke, her old cat, and bring her to the stones on Lantern Hill for midnight.

An early version: the game saves every two minutes, when you press Save, and when you leave the page; it picks up where you left off when you come back. Saves stay in this browser on this device. Built on the Strata engine; source.

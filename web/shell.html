Familiar

Halloween night, 2026, in the hill town of Hollowell. The sun is setting where it really sets at 42° north. Choose a costume and go trick-or-treating: knock on doors where the porch light is on, and have a joke ready. And tonight you can turn into a black cat: small and quick, able to jump onto fences, sheds and even roofs, slip through gaps, and nose up a latch from the inside. A cat can't knock or carry the candy bag, so the bag waits where you changed. And a cat sees the spirits out walking on the one night the veil is thin: the town's own dead, visiting.

The cat and the spirits are this game's fairy tale, not something Wiccans believe or do.

  • Mouse: look. WASD: walk, Shift: hurry.
  • 1–4 on Nana's walk: choose a costume. E: knock, pick up your bag, or open a latch. T: tell a joke when a neighbour asks for a trick. Q: turn into a cat, and back.
  • F1: the controls on screen. Esc: free the mouse.
  • Drag on the left half to walk (all the way out to hurry); drag on the right half to look.
  • Choose a costume with the buttons on Nana's walk. Knock at a door; Tell a joke when asked for a trick; Cat turns you into a cat and back. The big button changes to Pick up by your bag and Open at a latch.

An early version: lanterns, Nana's cider, Smoke, secrets and saves are on the way. The night starts over when you reload. Built on the Strata engine; source.

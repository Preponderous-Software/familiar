The game starts in the world, in first person on Nana's front walk at 5:20 pm on
Halloween, with the mouse captured for looking. A greeting and a help list
(controls) are on screen; F1 hides or shows the help.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| The mouse (`pt look DX DY`; about 7 pixels a degree) | look |
| W A S D | walk |
| Shift (`Shift_L`) | hurry |
| E | knock at a lit door, or use what is in front of you (light a lantern, pick, buy, stir, offer, open a latch) |
| T | tell a joke when a neighbour asks for a trick |
| 1 to 4 | choose a costume (only on Nana's walk, before the first knock) |
| Q | turn into a cat, and back (the cat climbs fences and roofs, squeezes through gaps and sees spirits, but cannot knock or carry the candy bag) |
| Tab | Wren's notebook (Left/Right turn its pages, Tab closes it) |
| M | sound on/off |
| F5 / F9 | save now / load the last save (it also autosaves every two minutes, and saves when you quit) |
| F1 | show or hide the help |
| Esc | free the mouse (Esc again quits the game, which saves first) |

Practical notes:

- **The clock runs in real time and cannot be paused.** One game minute passes
  every three seconds of play, so the whole night (5:20 pm to midnight) is
  about 20 minutes of play, and it keeps running while you read screenshots.
  Do not linger: act, then look. Porch lights go off at 9 pm. Report anything
  you could not do because time ran out, and say so plainly; it is a known
  property of this harness run, not necessarily a game bug.
- Turning is mouse-only: `pt look 300 0` turns about 40 degrees right,
  `pt look 0 100` tilts down. Relative motion is clamped at the window edge,
  so use several medium nudges rather than one huge one.
- The clock, your candy and what to do next are on the HUD; the notebook
  (Tab) holds what Wren knows about the night.
- There is no combat and no way to lose.
